# Decision Governance

- Important product uncertainty requires the official PRD workflow.
- Every new development task receives its own ADR through the official ADR skill.
- A routine task ADR may be concise, but still records approach, boundaries, alternatives, and related governing decisions.
- Draft PRDs and Proposed task ADRs do not authorize implementation.
- Only explicit human approval may move a governed decision to an accepted state.
- Superseded records remain in history and link to their replacements.
